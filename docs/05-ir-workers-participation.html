05 Workers' participation in management

DIRECTIVE PRINCIPLE Article 43A is a Directive Principle in Part IV of the Constitution. It is not a statute and not "in force" in the way a Code section is. Under Article 37 no court can enforce a Directive Principle, so 43A is a duty on the State. A worker cannot claim it as a right. The Labour Codes do not amend the Constitution, so the 43A answers below do not change.

IN FORCE The Industrial Relations Code 2020 (IR Code) is in force from 21 November 2025 (S.O. 5320(E)). It has no provision on workers' participation in management except the Works Committee in s.3. A Works Committee exists only where the appropriate Government orders one. Rule 5 of the IR (Central) Rules 2026 (G.S.R. 342(E)) says the employer must then proceed at once to constitute it.

IN FORCE The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code 2020 (OSH Code) is in force from 21 November 2025 (S.O. 5321(E)). Its s.22 gives the Safety Committee. How a Safety Committee is constituted depends on rules that this note does not cover.

REPEALED ID Act s.3, the old Works Committee rule, was repealed on 21 November 2025. The Code carries the same rule forward in s.3.

The Government schemes of 1958 to 1983 and the Bill of 1990 below are taught as history. This note does not state their present status.

Snapshot (keep these five)

  1. Article 43A is in Part IV (Directive Principles). It was inserted by the 42nd Amendment Act 1976, s.9, w.e.f. 3 January 1977. No court can enforce it (Art 37).
  2. Five levels, in order, as classified in Indian IR texts: information sharing, consultative, associative, administrative, decisive.
  3. Three thresholds, one look-alike trap: Works Committee 100 or more (IR Code s.3) IN FORCE; shop and joint councils 1975, 500 or more ; unit councils 1977, 100 or more .

1. Article 43A

"The State shall take steps, by suitable legislation or in any other way, to secure the participation of workers in the management of undertakings, establishments or other organisations engaged in any industry."

  • Article 43A was inserted by the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976, s.9.
  • It took effect w.e.f. 3 January 1977. The date comes from the official footnote in the Constitution. The Gazette notification itself was not reached.
  • It sits in Part IV, after Art 43 and before Art 43B.
  • Why it is not a worker's right: Art 37 says Directive Principles are not enforceable by any court.

2. Meaning, levels and forms

Meaning. Workers' participation in management means workers taking part in decision-making in the enterprise. One university course text links it to industrial democracy. No official source was found that uses the phrase.

Five levels, in order (as classified in Indian IR texts).

  1. Information sharing
  2. Consultative
  3. Associative
  4. Administrative
  5. Decisive

Text version: information sharing, then consultative, then associative, then administrative, then decisive. Some texts call level 1 "informative". No official source lists the five levels.

Forms.

Form What it is
By representation in committees Works committees and Joint Management Councils (JMCs). Shop councils and joint councils (1975 scheme) are also forms.
Board representation Worker-directors on the board.
Shop-floor, direct Quality circles and suggestion schemes. An ILO working document calls them direct, management-initiated participation.
Co-ownership Co-partnership with stock options, including employee stock option plans (ESOPs). The note does not define an ESOP in law.

3. Indian schemes in order

  1. Now Works Committee. The ID Act 1947 (s.3) had it. The IR Code s.3 has it now: 100 or more workers. IN FORCE
  2. 1958 Joint Management Councils (JMCs). Introduced in 1958 (Indian IR texts). The model agreement dates to 1957 (NCL 2002 para 12.7). The agreement was endorsed at the 15th Indian Labour Conference, say Indian IR texts. NCL 2002 para 12.7 says an All India Seminar in 1957 worked it out. Both say 1957.
  3. 1970 Workman director in banks. The Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970 (Act 5 of 1970) provides for a workman director on the board of each nationalised bank. The text was read from a non-official copy, so the section number is not given here. 1970 is the year of the Act. It is not the date the first worker-director sat.
  4. 1975 Shop councils and joint councils. Announced in 1975 (NCL 2002 para 12.26). Covers manufacturing and mining units with 500 or more workers, in the first instance. A Government publication links the scheme to the 20-point programme.
  5. 1977 Unit councils. For commercial and service organisations with large-scale public dealings (NCL 2002 para 12.30). Covers organisations with 100 or more persons (NCL 2002 para 12.31).
  6. Dec 1983 Scheme for central public sector undertakings. Notified in December 1983. Departmentally run central undertakings are left out because of the JCM (NCL 2002 para 12.33). Sources differ on the day of December (30 or 31), so learn the month only. Coaching notes call it the "comprehensive scheme"; no source read uses that name.
  7. 1990 Participation of Workers in Management Bill, 1990. Introduced in the Rajya Sabha on 30 May 1990. Withdrawn on 1 August 2017. Never enacted. Coaching notes say "lapsed". The Rajya Sabha record says "Withdrawn".

Text version of the timeline: Works Committee (ID Act 1947, now IR Code s.3); JMC 1958; bank workman director (Act of 1970); shop and joint councils 1975; unit councils 1977; central PSU scheme December 1983; Bill introduced 1990, withdrawn 2017.

Look-alike thresholds. The numbers 100 and 500 appear in different schemes, so learn each with its scheme name.

Scheme Year Threshold Covers
Works Committee ID Act 1947; IR Code s.3 now 100 or more workers Industrial establishment, by Government order
Shop councils and joint councils 1975 500 or more workers Manufacturing and mining, in the first instance
Unit councils 1977 100 or more persons Commercial and service organisations

Trap: shop councils (1975, 500) and unit councils (1977, 100) are both councils from the 1970s. Students swap their thresholds. One ILO working document gives 500 for unit councils. NCL 2002 para 12.31 gives 100, and NCL wins.

Where the IR Code stands. The IR Code has no participation provision other than the Works Committee (s.3). Art 43A is a directive to the State, and no court can enforce it (Art 37). The named exceptions are one workman director in each nationalised bank (the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970) and Works Committees ordered by the appropriate Government (IR Code s.3).

4. The Ahmedabad experiment

  • Who: A.K. Rice of the Tavistock Institute of Human Relations.
  • Where: the Calico Mills (Ahmedabad Manufacturing and Calico Printing Company), Ahmedabad.
  • Idea: socio-technical design. The loom shed was reorganised into small, internally structured and internally led work groups.
  • Term trap: coaching notes call these groups "autonomous". Rice's own term is "internally led"; it is taken from a scholarly review of his work, because his 1958 book was not read.

When. The experimental automatic loom shed was set up by March 1953 (Rice 1953). The Tavistock archive dates the initial changes to 1953 to 1954 and their measurement to 1970. Do not take 1953 to 1954 as the length of the whole experiment. Other sources give different spans, but they were seen only as search snippets and are not used here.

Result in Rice's 1953 paper (whole shed, 8 June to 22 August).

Measure Before After
Mean percentage efficiency 79.8 90.3
Mean percentage damage 31.8 24.5

Four cautions apply to these figures.

  • They come from one shed and one 1953 paper.
  • Rice counts "productivity" as higher efficiency and lower damage.
  • The works manager noted that the period was the hardest of the year.
  • The first phase raised damage.

Whether the gains lasted is not stated here.

5. Code and bridge block

Body Authority and section Rule Status
Works Committee IR Code s.3(1) The appropriate Government may, by general or special order, require an employer of an industrial establishment to constitute one. Trigger: 100 or more workers now, or on any day in the preceding 12 months. Worker representatives must be not less than employer representatives. IN FORCE Rule 5, IR (Central) Rules 2026. The manner of constitution is as prescribed.
Safety Committee OSH Code s.22(1) The appropriate Government may, by general or special order, require an establishment or class of establishments to constitute a Safety Committee of representatives of employers and workers. Worker representatives shall not be less than employer representatives. IN FORCE Manner of constitution needs rules
Canteen facility OSH Code s.24(1)(v) The employer must provide canteen facilities where 100 or more workers, including contract labourers, are ordinarily employed. Prescribed by the Central Government. IN FORCE
Canteen committee none The OSH Code text has no provision for a canteen managing or management committee. -
  • Both committees are orders, not automatic duties: the word is "may" require.
  • The IR Code does not use "participation" in this sense. Its Chapter II holds only s.3 (Works Committee) and s.4 (Grievance Redressal Committee).
  • The OSH Code does not use the word "participation" either.
  • The OSH Code gives a canteen facility, not a canteen committee. The old Factories Act canteen-committee rule is not taught here, because it was not verified.

Bridge. The ID Act s.3 (repealed) had the same Works Committee rule: 100 or more workmen in the preceding 12 months, and workmen's representatives not less than the employer's. REPEALED The IR Code s.3 carries it forward with "workers" for "workmen". IN FORCE

Q47 traps.

  • Students may pick (d) "workers' suggestion schemes". Suggestion schemes are a form of shop-floor participation (section 2), but the stem names three committees.
  • The publisher's explanation says participation is "individually or collectively". Keep that as the exam wording only. An Indian IR text says workers take part "not as individuals but as a group through their representatives". The ILO allows individual direct forms such as suggestion schemes. Learn the plain definition in section 2.

Answer: Article 43A, inserted by the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976, s.9, w.e.f. 3 January 1977. The Labour Codes do not change it. Basis:

Q2 (pattern: one relation; seed PYQ-EPFO-2012-Q62, adapted). Can a court order the State to secure workers' participation in management under Article 43A? Give the reason.

Answer: No. Art 43A is in Part IV, and under Art 37 the Directive Principles are not enforceable by any court. Basis:

Q3 (pattern: one attribute; seed PYQ-EPFO-2017-Q47, adapted to the Code). Under the IR Code, from what size of establishment can a Works Committee be ordered, and who sits on it?

Answer: 100 or more workers, now or on any day in the preceding 12 months (s.3(1)). The appropriate Government orders it. Worker representatives must be not less than employer representatives. IN FORCE Basis:

Q4 (pattern: look-alike; seed this note). Which scheme covers manufacturing and mining units with 500 or more workers, and which covers commercial and service organisations with 100 or more persons?

Answer: Shop councils and joint councils (1975) take 500 or more. Unit councils (1977) take 100 or more. Basis:

Answer: It was introduced in the Rajya Sabha on 30 May 1990 and withdrawn on 1 August 2017. It was never enacted. It did not "lapse". Basis:
