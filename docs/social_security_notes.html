Nine Acts on provident fund, state insurance, compensation, maternity, gratuity, building workers, unorganised workers and job vacancies became one Code on social security. This topic teaches the Code first, scheme by scheme: who is covered, the money numbers, the authority that decides, and the new gig, platform and unorganised worker rules. The old Acts appear only to show what changed.

Law today The Code on Social Security, 2020 Code on SS (Act 36 of 2020) is in force in full, by date. Code on SS s.142 (Aadhaar) has been in force from 3 May 2021 (S.O. 1730(E)). The Employees' Pension Scheme 1995 parts of Code on SS ss.15(3), 16(1)(a), 16(2), 164(2)(b), all of Code on SS s.16(1)(b), the Code on SS s.143 part at serial (v) and the Code on SS s.164(1) repeal of the matching EPF Act 1952 provisions are in force from 3 May 2023 (S.O. 2060(E)). Everything else is in force from 21 November 2025 (S.O. 5319(E), as corrected by S.O. 5936(E) of 19 December 2025). The Code repealed 9 Acts Code on SS s.164(1), including the EPF Act 1952. The Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026 (G.S.R. 344(E), 8 May 2026) are in force from the date of publication. A Rules chip marks a point that comes from the Rules, not the Code text. The EPF Scheme 2026 (G.S.R. 525(E)) and the EDLI Scheme 2026 (G.S.R. 526(E)) are dated 29 June 2026 and are in force on publication. The Employees' Pension Scheme 2026 (G.S.R. 527(E)) is dated 29 June 2026 and is also in force on publication.
  1. 1 Code, 9 Acts. The Code has 164 sections and 14 chapters. It was assented to on 28 September 2020. It is in force by date: 3 May 2021, 3 May 2023, and the rest from 21 November 2025.
  2. Who is covered. EPF: 20 or more employees Code on SS First Schedule. ESI: 10 or more persons, other than a seasonal factory. Gratuity: factories, mines, oilfields, plantations, ports and railway companies of any size, and shops or establishments with 10 or more employees. Once covered, always covered Code on SS s.1(8).
  3. The money numbers. EPF: the Code reads 10%, with 12% by notification Code on SS s.16(1)(a); the EPF Scheme 2026 fixes 12% each side EPF Scheme 2026 para 18(2). ESI: employer 3.25% plus employee 0.75% SS Central Rules r.19(1). Maternity: 26 weeks, or 12 weeks with two or more surviving children Code on SS s.60(3). Gratuity: 15 days' wages a year after 5 years Code on SS s.53. Death compensation: 50% of monthly wages x the factor Code on SS s.76(1)(a).
  4. The new groups. An aggregator pays 1% to 2% of annual turnover, capped at 5% of what it pays gig and platform workers Code on SS s.114(4); the duty starts on a notified date. Building workers' cess: 1% to 2% of the cost of construction Code on SS s.100(1). The Social Security Fund is in Code on SS s.141.
  5. Compliance. First-offence compounding Code on SS s.138(1): half of the maximum fine for a fine-only offence, three-fourths where there is imprisonment up to one year and fine. Obstructing an Inspector-cum-Facilitator: up to 6 months or Rs 50,000, or both. Exemption under Code on SS s.143: initially 3 years.

3. Employees' Provident Fund (Chapter III)

Law today. Code on SS Ch III covers EPF. Code on SS ss.15(1), 15(2) are in force from 21 November 2025; the Pension Scheme part of Code on SS s.15(3) from 3 May 2023, the rest from 21 November 2025. The EPF Act 1952 is repealed and the EPF Scheme 2026 is in force from 29 June 2026.

Schemes, contributions and funds

Code on SS s.15(1) Three Schemes

The Central Government may frame three Schemes by notification: the Employees' Provident Fund Scheme; the Employees' Pension Scheme (superannuation, retiring or permanent total disablement pension; widow or widower, children and orphan pension; nominee pension); and the Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance Scheme (life insurance). Code on SS s.15(2) lists the matters in Parts A, B and C of the Fifth Schedule.

Code on SS s.16(1)(a) Contribution

The employer's contribution to the Provident Fund is 10% of the wages payable to each employee, directly employed or through a contractor. The employee's contribution equals it.

old EPF Act 1952 s.6 10% of basic wages, dearness allowance and retaining allowance (if any). The base changed from named pay heads to "wages" Changed
Code on SS s.16(1)(a) 12% by notification

First proviso: the Central Government may, after inquiry, notify an establishment or class for which "ten per cent." reads "twelve per cent." The second proviso lets it notify the rate of employees' contributions and the period for any class of employee. An employee may, if he so desires, contribute more than 10% of the wages; the employer is not obliged to pay more than his own contribution.

Code on SS s.16(1)(b) Pension Fund

The Pension Fund receives from the employer's contribution not more than 8 and one-third per cent. of wages (or the notified per cent.), plus sums paid by employers of exempted establishments under Code on SS s.143, plus sums the Central Government specifies after appropriation by Parliament. In force from 3 May 2023.

Code on SS s.16(1)(c) Insurance Fund

The Deposit-Linked Insurance Fund receives from the employer, for every employee, an amount not more than 1% of the wages (or the notified per cent.), and a further sum not exceeding one-fourth of that contribution for the administration expenses of the Insurance Scheme, other than the expenses towards the cost of any benefits.

Recovery, assessment and appeals

  1. Authorised Officer An officer not below Group 'A' rank decides disputes on the applicability of Chapter III or IV and the amount due Code on SS s.125(1). No proceeding starts after 5 years from the date the dispute is alleged to have arisen or the amount is alleged to have been due.
  2. Inquiry It is held day to day as far as practicable, with an endeavour to conclude it within 2 years. If not concluded, the officer records reasons and the Central Provident Fund Commissioner may grant an extension of up to 1 year Code on SS s.125(2). The two years is an endeavour, not a bar.
  3. Interest and damages Simple interest at the notified rate Code on SS s.127. Damages not exceeding the arrears, after a hearing Code on SS s.128.
  4. Appeal to the Tribunal A person aggrieved by an order on (a) determination and assessment of dues under Code on SS s.125 for Chapter III and (b) damages under Code on SS s.128 for Chapter III may appeal to the Tribunal constituted by the Central Government Code on SS s.23(1). The employer must first deposit 25% of the amount due Code on SS s.23(3). The Tribunal endeavours to decide within 1 year Code on SS s.23(4).
  5. Recovery Officer The Authorised Officer or competent authority sends a certificate to the Recovery Officer Code on SS ss.129(2), 129(4). The Recovery Officer recovers arrears for any chapter of the Code, not for EPF alone, by attachment and sale of property, arrest of the employer or appointing a receiver.

Code on SS s.2(84) defines "Tribunal" as the Industrial Tribunal constituted by the appropriate Government under old ID Act 1947 s.7A. IR Code s.44(1) says the Industrial Tribunal constituted by the Central Government also exercises the jurisdiction of the Tribunal defined in the EPF Act.

16. Exemption and emergency powers

Law today. Code on SS s.143 (the parts not in force since 2023) and Code on SS s.144 are in force from 21 November 2025.

Code on SS s.143 Exemption

The appropriate Government may exempt an establishment or class of establishments from any provision of the Code or a scheme, if the employees receive benefits substantially similar or superior. The exemption is initially for three years from the date of the notification. An exempted establishment's PF is run by a board of trustees constituted by the employer. No exemption in respect of the PF, Pension or Insurance Scheme is granted or renewed without prior consultation with the Central Board. None in respect of Chapter IV without prior consultation with the Corporation.

Substantially similar or superior benefits; compliance with Chapter III (or the EPF Act 1952) or Chapter IV (or the ESI Act 1948) for a continuous three years before applying, without default; at least five hundred contributory members (Chapter III) or employees (Chapter IV); a cumulative member balance of Rs 50 crore or more for the PF or Pension Scheme; and majority employee consent for Chapter III. Old exemptions Code on SS s.164(2)(c): an exemption given under a repealed enactment stays in force till its validity expires or it ceases to operate under the Code. Separately, the EPF Scheme 2026 para 13(40) says establishments with exemption under the repealed Act apply for continuation within two years from the date of notification of the Social Security (Central) Rules 2026.

Students mix the two "threes". The exemption term is 3 years Code on SS s.143. The compliance record needed to apply is also 3 years, but it sits in the Rules SS Central Rules r.65(1).

Code on SS s.144 Emergency power

In a pandemic, endemic or national disaster, the Central Government may by order defer or reduce the employer's or employee's contribution, or both, under Chapter III or IV, for up to three months at a time, for the whole of India or a part. The statute says "endemic", not "epidemic".
